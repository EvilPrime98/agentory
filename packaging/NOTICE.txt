Agentory runs the `claude` (Claude Code), `spm` and `git` programs that are already installed on
your machine; it does not bundle them.

Built with GPUI (gpui-ce), terminal emulation by the `vt100` crate and PTY handling by `portable-pty`. Third-party crates keep their own licences.
